# Levels die in `PlanIsDead` (the death check), and NO — the chart gaps are a completely different fault

**LINE 1.** The levels were never missing: v1–v5 of `2026-08-16:ASIA` each held the same 6 good levels and each was killed **on arrival** by `activePlanIsDead`, the re-plan budget ran out, and `writeNoTradePlan` stored a `levels:null` plan as v6 — which the card rendered faithfully. The chart's flat weekend line is **unrelated**: NT8 hands us its own empty-minute placeholder bars. The two share no cause, and the dATR hypothesis linking them is **KILLED** (numbers below).

## Problem 1 — why every version died (two independent root causes)

**(a) The window predated the plan.** `activePlanIsDead` fed the full 2000×1m cache (~33h) into `PlanIsDead`, which asks "has every level been *touched* and *accepted through*". PDH/PDL/PDC are **by definition** prices the prior day traded through, and ONH/ONL the overnight session — so `levelTouched` over that window is a tautology and a classic day plan is dead the second it is written. Depth sweep: v1–v5 flip DEAD the instant the window reaches 976/979/984/990/996 trailing bars, i.e. back to Fri 05:00Z. **Fix `06f3343e`:** `PlanIsDeadSince` judges only bars opening at/after the plan's `created_at`; no post-plan evidence ⇒ not dead, because no evidence is not invalidation.

**(b) "2x5m" was counted on 1-minute bars.** `ClosesBeyond` counts *bars*, not minutes, so "two 5-minute closes" (10 min of acceptance) was decided by two ONE-minute closes — a proximity test, not a consumption test. Measured at v5's real death check (22:37:29Z), price 30193.50 sitting **inside** its own level stack, every level read consumed: PDL 997 closes above, PDC 31, ONL 31, EQL 1275 below, ONH 1276. **Fix `20a64c51`:** aggregate to the timeframe the rule names before judging; `aggregateToMinutes` emits one bar per **non-empty** bucket, so a closed market stays a gap.

**Also found and fixed:** the death check resolved the acceptance rule from whichever session was *live* rather than the plan's own (`20a64c51`) — benign at "2x5m" everywhere, fatal the moment a session sets "15m-close" (need=1 makes the loop strictly worse). And the executor prompt quoted a **hardcoded** re-plan cap of 2 while the card used the real resolver: the owner raised ASIA 2→4 mid-session (`strategies.updated_at` 22:25:11 UTC, the same second journald flipped "cap 2"→"cap 4"), so at v3 the card said "2 left" and the AI was told 0 (`8b24c85e`).

**Not fixed, deliberately:** the executor's entry facts (`EvaluateLevelFacts` → the prompt tail's "closes-beyond n, acceptance n/2") carry the *same* 1m-vs-rule mismatch. Changing it alters what the AI is told and the prompt goldens, so it needs its own decision. **Size S.** `death_condition` is written, stored, rendered — and evaluated by nothing; v5 died 47 points **above** the PDL its own death condition named. **Size M** (parse it, or stop asking the planner to write it).

**Fail loud (`f6521df9`):** the reason was sitting unread in `doc.reasoning` ("FAIL-CLOSED: re-plans exhausted…"). The card now always states *why* it has no levels, an emptiness with no reason names itself a fault rather than looking calm, and the mini chart is no longer gated on having levels — hiding it removed the one view that would have shown the market was fine and the **plan** was the problem.

## Problem 2 — the weekend flat line

**Not padding by us.** Every hop we own is a verbatim pass-through (AddOn copies NT8's arrays; `mergeBarsByTime` is a union over *received* bars; `/api/klines` and both chart surfaces are 1:1; lightweight-charts v5 has no continuous-axis mode to pad with). **NT8 itself** materialises its empty-minute placeholder records as bars at the `.ncd` file's base price with zero volume. Byte proof: `MNQ 09-26/20260814.Last.ncd` = 4346 B = 32-byte header + exactly 1438 byte-identical 3-byte records, base price at header offset 12 = **30147.50**; the healthy 08-13 file has 3011 distinct records. Live proof: 959 of 1500 1m bars flat at exactly 30147.50, volume 0, contiguous 08-14 00:02→16:00 CT, reproduced on 5m/15m/1h. `ES 09-26` is poisoned identically.

**Fix `47dbb269`:** refused at ingest (`SeedHistorical` + `Upsert`) — the one place that protects the chart **and** the detectors — with `DroppedPlaceholders()` so the condition is observable. The test is narrow (zero volume **and** zero range), so a thin-but-real minute and a doji with volume both survive. Weekend-boundary fixture asserts no bar inside the closed period, a straight step from the last Friday bar to the first Sunday bar with original timestamps, and no flat segment.

**Session tagging is already metadata-only — verified, not assumed.** All six `CMESessionDayKey`/`ActiveSession` sites are scalar/aggregate SELECTs; the only function returning a filtered `[]Kline` filters on `CloseTime < now`, never session. Detector output is byte-identical with ASIA/LONDON enabled, default, or all disabled.

**The dATR hypothesis is KILLED, twice.** `ActivePlanLevels` has exactly two production callers (the W7 level-state writer and the executor's prompt tail) — neither touches what the planner *stores*, and `planLevelFacts` iterates `doc.Levels` raw with no dATR term anywhere in the card path. And the arithmetic refuses too: measured dATR = **60.625**, band ±90.94, all six ASIA v5 levels inside (worst = PDL at 46.25, 2.0× headroom); dATR would have to fall 49% to drop even one. **Deleting every padded bar changes dATR by exactly 0.0000** — `DailyATRProxy` is a per-session-day (max High − min Low), which flat bars *inside* an established range cannot move, and a fully flat day is dropped by its `hi > lo` guard rather than averaged in as a zero.

**Correction to my own commit message (`47dbb269`), and it is append-only so it stands as written:** I attributed Friday's destroyed file to the watchdog livelock overwriting it during the session. The storm's Friday phase began 17:15:40 CT — *after* the 16:00 close — so it never overlapped the 00:02–16:00 dead window. What is certain: both files carry mtime Sun 13:07, minutes after NT8 restarted at 13:06:40, and were re-fetched on reconnect rather than recorded live.

## Problem 3 — ITEM 15, the version chips

**(a) Dead: NEVER-BUILT, and it was a written acceptance criterion (D16).** `VersionChips` took one prop, `version: number`, and *fabricated* its labels from the count — it had never seen a list of versions, only how many there were. Each chip was a bare `<span>`: no `onClick`, `role` or `tabIndex`. Downstream was equally cold: `getPlanHistory` had zero callers, `handlePlanHistory` deliberately strips `.Doc`, and `PlanStore.GetPlan` — the one method that could serve an old version — had **zero callers of any kind, tests included**. Meanwhile the data was all there: `plans` is append-only, every version keeps its own doc, lifecycle, `trigger_reason`, `model_id` and `created_at`. Pure missing read path. **Built:** `ListVersions`, `GET /plan/versions` (with `death_reason`, `superseded_by`, and a plain-language `diff_vs_next`), and `?version=N` on `/plan/today` — the same handler, so a historical view resolves overlays, facts and degradation identically, and owner overlays render on the version they belonged to for free (`745f84f7`, `f630ceea`). The **door is closed** on a historical view: every mutating endpoint writes the LATEST version, so an edit made while reading v2 would land on v6 — the W15.C hazard on a second axis.

**(b) Overlap does not exist, and I will not claim to have fixed it.** The chips *collapsed* at n≥4 to "v1 … vN", so at v6 the owner saw **three** boxes, not six; measured live at 390×844, every inter-chip gap is exactly +4.00px with nothing absolutely positioned, negatively margined or transformed. What genuinely produces "stacked and unreadable", all now fixed: the inactive chip painted at **2.83:1** contrast (below the 4.5:1 AA floor) with 1.20:1 borders, so three chips read as one grey smear; the header row had no `flex-wrap`, overflowing the 316px card interior by **~41px** at 390px and pushing the ✎/💬 buttons off the card; the ellipsis **hid v2–v5 with no way to reach them**; active-vs-inactive was signalled by colour alone, against `chips.tsx`'s own stated rule; tap targets were ~24×29px.

**Overlay orphaning — reported, not fixed.** Overlays are keyed `(plan_id, plan_version)` and every reader resolves against the LATEST, so a re-plan strands them silently. The real fix is a **rebase** with a pre-image guard — an index-based patch (`/levels/3`) cannot be re-pointed at a doc whose level array changed; done wrong it moves an owner's edit onto a *different* level, which is worse than dropping it. **Size M.** What shipped is the honesty: a P1 alert naming how many edits a re-plan is about to strand (`499dd59f`). Verified severity: live in code, **never fired** — `plan_overlays` holds exactly one row and it is demo-seeder output. The first real owner edit is the one at risk.
