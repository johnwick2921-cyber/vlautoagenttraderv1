## 3. One month: test structural-target-first admission within the existing method

**The one project is a prospective, paired test of target ownership and feasibility.** [I] Primary hypothesis: choosing the nearest predeclared opposing structural objective before checking the unchanged 2.0 ratio improves **net dollars per eligible opportunity** compared with using the model's authored arm target. ### 3.1 Fixed treatment and comparison

**Population:** prospectively authored **NY `reject` limit opportunities**, both sides, after all shared non-target eligibility requirements but **before either target's R:R check**. NY is a fixed research scope for data and clock consistency, not a claim of superiority from its 21 trades. Identity is contract + CME day + formation-known level identity + side + first qualifying confirmation/approach episode. A new plan version alone does not create a new opportunity. End the episode at documented invalidation, session expiry, or completed execution; a later episode needs a fresh price-state transition, not a new ID. Record every candidate, including formation-unknown and operationally invalid ones in a separate exclusion ledger.

**Baseline A:** retain the model's `arm.target`, authored entry, composed initial stop, confirmation, current limit-placement rules, all shared gates, one-contract ceiling, suspended BE/trail, and existing news/EOD exits. `target_chain` is not substituted for `arm.target` because their authority differs (`kernel/planner_prompt.go:722`). Freeze the complete successful planner input/output and policy hash before subsequent bars are examined.

**Candidate B:** change only the target/admission policy. From the **same frozen seated map**, take the first boundary encountered in the trade direction, excluding the entry level and same-price duplicates: for a long, the closest strictly higher level price or zone's lower edge; for a short, the closest strictly lower level price or zone's upper edge. A straddling zone means no clean room and a skip. Use a zone only if its edges and formation time were known; otherwise record missing provenance. Round the target toward entry to the instrument tick. Eligible boundary kinds are those already seated in the frozen input, with their actual provenance labels; no new profile/order-flow interpretation or post-read level is added. This is a reproducible geometric hypothesis, not a declaration that every boundary reverses price.

If there is no valid boundary, missing formation provenance, or the candidate fails the **unchanged 2.0 floor using the composed stop and permitted entry**, B skips. It must not jump to a farther boundary, tighten the stop, or change the trigger to pass. Revalidation follows the frozen current placement rules and logs every new accepted stop; no changed stop silently inherits the earlier ratio. The nearest-boundary rule is chosen now, not fitted to historical extrema. Failure to find feasible geometry is a substantive result about compatibility of this method's shape, not an excuse to choose another target after seeing prices.

### 3.2 Measurement and execution discipline

This audit delivers the protocol only. A subsequent authorized project would collect the missing evidence and evaluate isolated shadow/SIM paths; it must never put both alternatives on the production broker book. Use identical timestamped market input, costs, latency assumptions and legal execution semantics. For each opportunity record A/B target, stop, gate decision, fill/nonfill, invalidation, accepted initial risk, both exit sequences, all costs and result in dollars. Position MAE/MFE proxies are not ordered paths.

Use two distinct outputs. The **primary paired opportunity test** prices each independently eligible opportunity once; a skipped/nonfilled variant contributes zero trading P&L, with cost only if actually incurred under the cost model. It answers target/admission value per common opportunity. The **deployable policy simulation** separately runs each variant chronologically with its own one-position/pending-order state; shorter holding can change later participation. Report daily dollars including eligible days with zero trades. Do not sum overlapping isolated opportunities and present that as a one-contract portfolio return.

Include contract-specific bid/ask or tick/order events, actual broker-accepted triggers/stops, cost schedule and ack/fill times. A bar that spans both stop and target without known ordering yields lower/upper feasible outcomes; a limit touch without known fillability remains uncertain. Retain those opportunities in the denominator and show bounds rather than delete inconvenient rows. Advancement requires the conclusion to survive conservative ordering/nonfill/cost bounds. Extra **$2/$4/$8 per executed round trip** are predeclared stress assumptions, not measured commissions; skip variants incur no hypothetical execution charge. Unknown economically unbounded paths mean **UNMEASURABLE**, not zero P&L.
