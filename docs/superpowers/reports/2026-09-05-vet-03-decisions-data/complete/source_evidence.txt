docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1829:     that: a longer window delays the wrong answer, it does not make the evidence
docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1830:     exist.
docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1831:     **Probe:** for every predicate that concludes something is DEAD, GONE, DONE or
docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1832:     STALE, ask what the healthy case emits. If the healthy case is also silent,
docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1833:     the predicate cannot distinguish them and its threshold is decoration. Then

docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1836:     are the same branch, and the destructive one wins by default.

docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1838:     observation from the system that owns the fact — here the broker's own book
docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1839:     (F12's `order_snapshot`), which answers whether or not anything happened.

docs/superpowers/AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1843:     rows to ASK about and never decides the answer.
