## ONE-PAGE SUMMARY

**Verdict on the level subsystem.** The map is well built and badly measured. The detectors are clean, deterministic and cheap; the seating machinery has more guarantees than a desk of six would ever need. But the one instrument that was supposed to tell the owner which levels matter — the live D1′ touch corpus — is contaminated three ways in code that shipped 36 hours ago, and every number anyone has quoted from it (48.8 % first-touch hold, RTH-L breaking 68 %, the "retire six kinds" list) is an artifact of that contamination, not a property of the tape. When I re-run the same detector honestly over the full persisted tape, **no kind and no family separates from a random-level null of 0.506, in either direction** — including RTH-L, which holds 0.628, not breaks 0.68, and including the prior-day family, whose apparent edge is 57 double-counted episodes (deduplicated 0.562 [0.504, 0.617], n=292). The prior day's high and low, the two anchors every trader on NQ watches, are computed from a calendar-day bucket read off a 33-hour ring, and differ from the session-day truth on six of the eight days I could check — by 37–218 points on five of them. And the seating is not a filter: 92 % of seats are grade A, twelve of twelve seats fill in 14 of the 15 recorded reads, and a fifth of the seats (217 of 985) sit more than 100 points from price, where they are reached one time in three — the 8 % that sit beyond 200 points are reached one time in fifteen. **Nothing in this section is ready to be traded as a "level edge"; the honest work is to fix the instrument and let the tape grow.**

**The three biggest problems.**

1. **The live touch corpus cannot be used, and it is already being used [A].** `trader/detector_record.go:57` scans the whole 2,000-bar void scope (~33 h, `kernel/svp.go:46-47`) for every seated level on every planner read, but the idempotency watermark (`store/touch_outcomes.go:254-266`) and the ordinal (`:73-84`) are scoped to `opened_at_ms >= <current CME session-day start>`. So every episode that opened before 17:00 CT the prior evening is re-recorded, with ordinal 1, on every read — until that price records its first episode opening *after* the session-day start, which raises the watermark and silences them (`NewEpisodesSince`, `kernel/detector_recorder.go:99-107`). Dynamic lines never get there, because their price changes every read and the price-keyed watermark never matches. **677 rows are 423 distinct episodes; RTH-L's 140 rows are 14 episodes of one price copied up to 11 times** (`q06`). Worse, nothing cuts the scan at the level's formation time (`DetectedLevel.FormedAtMs`, `kernel/levels.go:89`, is never read by the recorder): **all 14 RTH-L episodes opened between 22:10 CT 09-02 and 08:36 CT 09-03 — 13 of them before the 08:36 bar that printed 29199.25, and all 14 before that price could be a *prior*-day RTH low (which requires the 09-03 RTH to be complete, 14:45 CT)** (`q10`, `r02`). Dynamic lines (VWAP, swings, OR) are scanned at today's price over yesterday's bars. And `BuildCandidatePool` (`kernel/detector_recorder.go:67-90`) writes `score 0.0, grade ''` for every cut row although the graded pool was in hand — a fabricated zero (canon "no fabricated values"). `research-conformance.md:243-300` and `veteran-review.md:82-103` both computed rates from this table.
2. **PDH/PDL/PDC are the wrong numbers three separate ways [A].** (a) `kernel/levels_multiday.go:146-160` buckets by CT *calendar* day, so at the 08:00 NY read "yesterday" includes 17:00–23:59 of the current Globex session: on 08-27 the engine's PDH 29655.75 was 218.5 pts above the session-day PDH 29437.25 — it was the overnight high (`q11_replay.txt`, "calendar-day vs session-day"); the two definitions differ on **6 of 8** measurable days, by 37–218 pts on five of them (−182.75, +78.75, +218.5/+37.5, +80.5, +42.5) and by 1.75 on the sixth. (b) At the 16:30 CT ASIA read the prior *calendar* day is two days back and the RTH that closed 90 minutes earlier is excluded — this one is established by the code alone (`todayCal` at `:46`, `mostRecentPriorDay` at `:229-237`, `k < todayCal`) **[A]**; the store corroborates on 3 of the 13 ASIA-read PDH/PDL rows (08-24 PDH, 08-25 PDL, 08-27 PDH match D-2 exactly, **none** matches D-1), the other 10 being Sunday reads that correctly carry Friday's extremes or ring-truncated values (`q14`). (c) The bucket is built from the 2,000-bar ring, and the 900-bar coverage guard admits a bucket missing up to **8 h** (1,380 − 900 bars): at the 09-02 13:58 CT read the engine wrote a PDH/PDL/PDC-family row at 29317.25 (`level_state` carries no per-kind label, so the PDH identification is **[B]** from the row plus **[A]** from the tape: 09-01's high from 04:58 CT onward is exactly 29317.25 on 1,082 bars ≥ 900, while the full calendar day's high was 29571 at 00:xx CT, outside the ring) — and that number was carried into the 09-02 ASIA plan as "PDH" (`q15`).
3. **Seating does not rank and the grade does not gate [A].** Across all 254 plans (2,391 seated rows) grades are A 1,920 (80.3 %) / B 152 / C 319 (`q12a`); deduplicated to one row per level-day, **A = 906 of 985 (92 %)**. Twelve of twelve seats fill in **14 of the 15** recorded detector reads (12 plan-versions; the 01:30 CT LONDON v0 read seated 11) (`q04`, `r01`). 217 of 985 seated levels (22 %) sat >100 pts from price at seat and were reached 31.3 % of the time — 44.7 % in the 100–200 pt band, 6.6 % beyond 200 (`q12b`, `r05`). Downstream, nothing consumes level grade: not `trader/entry_gate.go`, `kernel/entry_law.go`, `kernel/plan_confirm.go`, `kernel/arm_kind.go` nor `trader/armed_executor.go` (grep, zero hits); grade changes only seat order, the `min_grade` cut (which Tier-1 kinds are exempt from, `levels_score.go:70-84`) and prompt text.

**The three biggest opportunities.**

1. **Fix the instrument, then let time do the work [A/T].** Three code changes (formation-time scan start, full-window watermark, persisted real scores for cut rows) make `touch_outcomes` and `candidate_pool` the tables the 09-03 wave designed — and the third of them is not a proposal at all: class 64 already legislates that the pool records cut levels *with their propensities* (`AUDIT-CHECKLIST.md:1402-1405`), and the shipped writer does not. That one needs no ruling, only the fix. The replay's pre-registered n ≥ 200 per-kind floor is the right bar; nothing in this section clears it and nothing will for weeks. Every hour spent re-weighting `levels_score.go` before that is fitting noise — the 09-02 replay said so and I agree with it.
2. **Two things worth pre-registering — and I have talked myself down on one of them [T, no verdict].** (a) A lost prior low re-tested *from below* holds 0.680 [0.484, 0.828] (n=25, RTH-L). I called this "the flip" in my first draft; it does not survive its own audit. The PDL cell that looked like a second observation is **29 of the same 31 episodes**, two level-days supply 21 of the 25 resolutions, it is **one of 41 entry-side cells with n ≥ 15** (eight of them read ≥ 0.62), and the RTH-H mirror runs the *other* way (classic-side 0.654 n=26 beats flip-side 0.579 n=19; pooled flip 0.607 n=56 vs classic 0.569 n=58). That is the expected maximum of 41 draws around a coin, not a signal. Pre-register it, expect nothing. (b) EQH first touch holds **0.711 [0.566, 0.823] (n=45)** on the plan-forward tape while EQL holds 0.473 (n=55) — this interval clears 0.5 *and* the replay null's upper bound 0.517, on a kind that passes my own n ≥ 30 floor. It is still one of ~25 plan-forward kind cells and there is no plan-forward null to judge it against, so it is a measurement I would fund, not a trade.
3. **Seat by reachability, not by decoration [T on one day / I].** Deduplicated to distinct levels (the pool's 360 rows are 84 levels read 15 times on **one** day, 09-04): levels that were ever seated were touched 42 of 55 = 76.4 %, cut levels 6 of 29 = 20.7 %, and the difference is distance (91 vs 195 pts mean), not grade (`r04`). What tightening the band actually buys is smaller than I first wrote: of 985 plan seats, 887 (90 %) already sit inside ±175 pts (≈0.5×dATR) and the 98 outside it were reached **six times**, so a ±0.5×dATR executor band raises the touched-in-session share from 79.1 % to 87.1 % and costs six touches (`r05`) — worth doing for the executor's attention, not the "third more of the map" my first draft claimed.

## 2 · WHICH KINDS I WOULD SEAT, WHICH THE TAPE SEATS, AND WHOM I TRUST

**From experience [I].** On NQ the levels that matter are the ones other people's orders are parked at, and those are boringly conventional: the prior *Globex session* high/low/close (17:00→16:00, not a calendar day), the overnight high/low (as the first liquidity of the RTH day), the opening range (5 and 15/30 min), the initial balance, the session VWAP, the prior settlement, and the prior day's value area. Round hundreds are a tie-break, never a seat. Zones (OB/FVG/S/D) are where I *expect* a reaction to begin, not where I rest an order; equal highs/lows are where I expect a stop run to end. I would never seat more than the day can reach.

**What the tape says [T].** Pooled by family on the replay, **deduplicated on `(day, price, opened_ms)`** — which matters, because my first draft's prior-day family counted 57 episodes twice (PDH_cal == PDH on 08-26 and 08-28; RTH-L == PDL on 08-24, 08-31 and 09-02, the same price wearing two kind labels): prior-day **0.562 [0.504, 0.617] (n=292, 39 level-days)** — raw 0.570 [0.517, 0.622] (n=335) — open 0.531 [0.450, 0.610] (n=145), profile 0.509 (n=214), VWAP family 0.503 (n=189), round 0.514 (n=1,155), **overnight 0.439 [0.349, 0.534] (n=107)**. My first draft called prior-day "the only family whose lower bound clears the null, by 0.011"; that 0.011 was double counting (`r03`). The overnight family's point estimate is the lowest, consistent with ONH/ONL being *liquidity* (sweep, then decide) rather than support/resistance, which is exactly the role the grammar already gives them (`levels_role.go:36`). **The one cell that made me sit up — and then made me sit back down [T, n=25, no verdict].** Split by the side price *came from*: RTH-L approached from **below** (price already under the prior low, rallying back into it) holds 17/8 → 0.680 [0.484, 0.828]; from above (the classic fade-long) 10/8 → 0.556 [0.337, 0.754]. Four things say it is noise, and I found them by auditing my own draft: (i) the PDL cell I called an "identical pair" is **29 of the same 31 episodes** — one price, two kind labels, not a second observation; (ii) the cell spans four level-days and **two of them supply 21 of the 25 resolutions** (08-31 10/4, 08-24 6/1); (iii) it is **one of 41 entry-side cells with n ≥ 15**, of which eight read ≥ 0.62 — 0.68 on n=25 is the expected maximum of that many draws around a coin; (iv) the RTH-H mirror runs the *other* way: its classic side (from below, into resistance) holds 0.654 (n=26) against the flip side's 0.579 (n=19), and pooled across the four prior-day anchors flip 0.607 (n=56, 14 level-days) barely differs from classic 0.569 (n=58, 17 level-days). **Where experience and tape disagree, and whom I trust.** The tape says nothing separates; experience says PDH/PDL/ONH/ONL/OR/IB/VWAP/settlement earn their seats. I trust experience *for the structure of the map* and the tape *for any claim of edge*, and the distinction matters: these levels are coordination points — their value is that everyone else is looking at them — and that is not a hold-rate claim, it is a claim about where volatility and decisions cluster (and here I have to correct myself: **Osler 2000** — *Support for Resistance*, FRBNY Economic Policy Review 6(2) — tested exactly whether published S/R levels bounce more than arbitrary ones in intraday FX and found they do, while **Osler 2003** (J. Finance 58(5)) explained it with order clustering: take-profits at round numbers reverse trends, stop-losses just beyond them accelerate through. My draft said Osler was about "where stops sit, not that they hold", which is wrong; and my RN result — 0.514 [0.485, 0.543] on n=1,155 at a ±16-pt band that merges Osler's *at* and *just-beyond* regions — is a **[T] disagreement with Osler 2003 on MNQ**, not a restatement of him [R]). A 0.55 hold rate at PDL is not why I seat PDL; I seat it because when price gets there, something happens, and the plan needs a name for the place. What the tape *does* license is the negative: round numbers are null on n=1,155 and should not cost seats; MID-O and SETT never seat and lose nothing; a zone kind that generates 117 candidates for 8 seats (OB) is a candidate-pool tax.
