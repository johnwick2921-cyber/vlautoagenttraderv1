## Atomic accounting

[A source/tests] PositionStore.ApplyNT8Exit stores the complete receipt, exact account/symbol/side/trader-owned position decrement, actual execution fill and realized PnL in one database transaction. Duplicate receipt IDs have no additional effect; inconsistent payloads, zero/missing/fractional/oversized quantities, invalid prices, ambiguous ownership and mismatched bracket lineage refuse without an applied receipt. SQL compare-and-update prevents stale full-row overwrites. Injection of a fill-insert failure rolls back all three records; concurrent repeated receipt delivery reduces once. Agent19's actual callback interleave test reads an entry row, executes ApplyNT8Exit, then applies cumulative entry growth: residual quantity and PnL survive both transactions.

An exit before entry evidence remains a complete durable pending receipt. Generic receipts cannot consume a position whose entry timestamp is later than the exit. Exact immutable SL/TP entry lineage allows delayed entry materialization despite its processing timestamp being later. Pending receipts are retried around legitimate position reconciliation; no missing entry or quantity is invented.

OPEN Quantity is residual quantity and EntryPrice is residual cost basis. CLOSED Quantity retains the established historical total entry quantity convention; per-execution fill rows always contain actual exit quantity. Cumulative EntryNotional is nullable: legacy unknown is NULL. Cumulative entry updates after partial exits use actual incremental entry notional and SQL residual weighting; unknown legacy post-exit notional refuses rather than fabricating cost basis. On final exit, known total notional/EntryQuantity restores historical average entry price without changing accumulated actual realized PnL. Futures quantities must be whole contracts; prices remain unrounded execution prices; PnL is signed price delta × actual contracts × FuturesPointValue (MNQ $2/point). Existing fees persist, and supplied receipt fees are added once. Current exit wire carries no commission, so zero additional recorded exit commission is unknown/unreported commission, not a measured zero-cost assertion.

## Execution identity and account truth

[A source/tests] Additive `position_close.exit_order_id` comes from actual C# OrderEventArgs.Order.OrderId. SignalID remains correlation. Receipt identity uses account, root symbol, held side and broker order ID, independent of echo sequence. Two manual orders named Close retain separate identities; one retransmission with changed sequence does not reduce twice. Legacy frames without the new field accept UUID operation names or known bot bracket lineage; generic manual names refuse. Broker IDs are assumed unique per account/order lifetime; no reception-time pseudo-ID is used. Conflicting reuse is an explicit error. A completed order frame represents its cumulative filled quantity, not a per-execution stream; C# still emits completed Filled exits only. Positive-filled terminal cancel of an EXIT remains outside this completed-exit wire boundary, unlike repaired cumulative ENTRY terminal receipts.

[A source/tests] Own-row completion no longer creates an account-flat confirmation marker. Account positions and their local receive timestamp are read atomically. Missing/null arrays remain unknown; explicit [] means computed empty. After an accepted/pending exit, positions must be received strictly after its local millisecond watermark, no more than 60 seconds old and not future-dated. The watermark is persisted with the receipt and loaded on close/reconcile startup. Unknown position reads propagate through ntHeldPosition and actual executeOpenLongWithRecord, preventing a new order. Same-millisecond snapshots conservatively wait for the next heartbeat. A new process must receive fresh positions; local clock changes can prolong refusal. Broker timestamps are not trusted as local receipt order. Hook/stop-loss notifications run only once at owned-row terminal completion, separately from account-flat admission.
