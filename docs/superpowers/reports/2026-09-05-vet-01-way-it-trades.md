## SUMMARY — one page

**Verdict for this section.** As it stands the book is a **short-biased level-fade book** — 58 trades in 12 CME session-days (17:00 CT roll), 4.8 a day, one MNQ contract — that wins **32.1 %** of decided trades (18/56, Wilson [21.4, 45.2]) and is paid **1.75 : 1** on the winners, for **−$8.04 a trade, 95 % CI [−35.04, +18.95]** (sample sd 104.9), gross of commission (`revise/r01_headline.out`). Break-even at a 1.75 payoff is a 36.4 % win rate; the book runs **four points** under it. Nothing in the tape proves an edge; nothing rules one out in exactly one cell — **reject-condition fades (n = 31: 14 W / 16 L / 1 flat, +$586, 46.7 % [30.2, 63.9])** — and everything the system does that is not that cell loses. The shape (resting limit at a seated level, stop = 1.5×ATR5m, target written by the model to clear a 2.0 R:R gate, flat 14:45) **does not make money on this tape as configured**, and the reason is arithmetic rather than luck: the planned target sits at **3.44 × ATR5m** (p50) while the tape's favourable excursion is **1.03 × ATR5m** (p50) and **2.47** at p80 (n = 61) — **8 of 59** trades ever travelled as far as their own target, **10 of 58** exits happened at the target and **34 of 58** at the stop. I do not claim the shape *cannot* work; I claim this configuration's reward side is unreachable, and I have replayed the alternatives (Q4). **[T] n = 58** (sensitivity, n = 65: 21/42/2, Σ −$563.93, mean −$8.68, payoff 1.62, 4.8 points under break-even).

**Three biggest problems.**

1. **The reward is written to the gate, not to the tape.** 47 of 132 armed scenarios since 08-15 carry a planned R:R inside [2.00, 2.30) — 35.6 % [28.0, 44.1] (`q11_planned_rr.out`) — and the prompt literally tells the model the floor and the stop floor (`kernel/planner_prompt.go:733` "R:R … must be ≥ 2.0 (ARM_MIN_RR) AND the stop distance must be ≥ 1.5× the current 5m ATR"; `kernel/class45_feeds_forward.go:199` "author stops AND targets consistent with it, or your R:R will not survive the widening") **[A]**. A gate on a number the model controls is an instruction to write a bigger number.
2. **The entry that produced 81 % of the book is the worst entry.** Decision-path market entries: 47 trades, 12 W / 33 L / 2 F, **−$560.93**, 26.7 % [16.0, 41.0]; the `1x5m_close` confirm class alone is 29 trades, 5 W / 23 L / 1 F, **−$1,135**, 17.9 % [7.9, 35.6]. Arm fills: 11 trades, 6 W / 5 L, +$94.50 (`revise/r13_paths.out`). Strict mode closed that door on 09-03; the historical book is that door. **[T]**
3. **It trades every tape the same, and the dead tape pays the bill.** ASIA: 16 trades, 2 W / 13 L / 1 F, **−$552.43** (13.3 % [3.7, 37.9]), mean R −0.52; the low-ATR5m tercile (ATR ≤ 22.57 pts, cuts re-taken on the compliant 55 with an ATR): 19 trades, 3 W / 16 L, **−$772.00**, mean R −0.48. Mid + high terciles together: 36 trades, +$110.07 (`revise/r12_tables.out`). The code registry ships ASIA and LONDON `Enabled: false` (`kernel/session_registry.go:93,102`; NY `Enabled: true` at `:114`) and the bound strategy's `sessions[]` turns both back on — deliberately, by a coded precedence rule (`trader/auto_trader_planconfig.go:88-101`, `sessionRunnable` :104-121) **[A]**. n < 30 everywhere, so "no verdict" by the rule — but the sign of every dead-tape cell is the same. The competing lever is side, not session: longs 19 trades **−$808.50**, shorts 39 **+$342.07**, and shorts are positive in LONDON (+$250.00) and NY (+$487.00) and negative only in ASIA (−$394.93).

**Three biggest opportunities.**

1. **Take the target away from the model — but not to a percentile I picked off 29 trades.** I ran the replay this page previously only asked for (`revise/r07_replay.py`, target = k × ATR5m, stop unchanged, a trade takes the target iff its recorded MFE reached it). It refutes the draft's own prescription: at the reject cell's true p60 (1.835×) the cell gives back $30 and the whole book $27 — nothing — and at the **1.4–1.5×** the draft actually quoted, the reject cell falls from **+$586.00 to +$420.62 / +$472.35**. The surface is non-monotone in k (whole book, n = 58: k = 1.0 → **+$36.95**, 1.2 → **+$49.33**, 1.4 → −$227.88, 1.835 → −$493.73, 2.5 → −$199.23) — which is what n = 29–31 looks like, and no k should be picked from it. What survives is the *structural* half: the target must come from something the model does not control — **the next seated opposing level** — and R:R must be a filter against that number, not a number for the model to satisfy. That replay is still owed (§Q4 candidate 2 is the only untested one). Note the arithmetic before anyone builds it: a 1.5×ATR5m stop floor × a 2.0 R:R filter demands a 3.0×ATR5m target, and **only 5 of 61** trades ever showed 3.0×ATR of favourable excursion — keeping both is a no-trade rule. What I would watch: target-hit share (now 10/58 = 17.2 % [9.6, 28.9]) and payoff (1.75).
2. **A no-progress scratch — replayed, and it holds.** Losers die at the stop (38 of 41 with MAE ≥ 0.95 × stop [80.6, 97.5]) after a median 21 minutes; 21 of 41 never printed 0.5 × stop of favourable excursion. I replayed "MFE < 0.3 × stop at 15 minutes → scratch at that bar's close" over the era's 1m bars (`revise/r07_replay.py`): **8 trades scratched, book −$466.43 → −$130.50, +$335.93, and not one winner touched.** The whole 5 × 3 grid (10/15/20/30/45 min × 0.2/0.3/0.5 × stop) improves the book in **15 of 15 cells**; only the 10-minute and 0.5× corners cost winners. My path recompute agrees to the tick with the store's independently written `mfe` on the rows I checked (526, 527, 540, 547, 589), which is the check that makes the replay believable. In-sample on 55 trades with bar coverage, so this is a candidate, not a knob — but it is now the best-evidenced item on the page.
3. **Gate the session and the vol regime by a measured switch, and fix the detector writer's watermark before ruling on any level kind.** The D1′ corpus in the store is 677 rows but only **370 distinct touches**, so every per-kind number ever quoted from it — including the prior review's "RTH-L breaks 68 % (n = 63)" — is inflated 1.8×. The mechanism is narrower than the draft said and the fix is one line of scope: a dedupe watermark exists (`trader/detector_record.go:61-62` → `kernel/detector_recorder.go:99-107`) but is filtered to the current CME session day (`store/touch_outcomes.go:254-266`, `dayMs = kernel.CMESessionDayStart(now)`, 17:00 CT), while the detection scope spans days — so an episode that opened on a PRIOR session day is re-written on every read **[A]**. The store shows exactly that: of the 158 episodes that opened inside the current session day, **158 are singletons**; every duplicated group opened earlier (`revise/r14_dedup.out`). Deduplicated, the only kind with n ≥ 30 is VWAP (56 hold / 37 break, 0.602 [0.501, 0.696]); RTH-L is 4 / 9, n = 13 — **no verdict**.

**The one thing I would stop everything for on Monday** is in SURPRISES, not in the recommendations, because I was told not to act on surprises: on 09-04 between 10:38 and 10:53 CT the NT8 SIM book held **nine identical resting sell-stop entries** for a one-contract system while the ledger called each of them cancelled (`q18_nt8_working.out`, `q22_0904_arms.out`).
