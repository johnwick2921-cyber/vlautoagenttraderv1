## S4b(3) — the oppose-D short penalty by month (era-artifact check)

Two facts. First, the penalty is one month: the gate's oppose-D short cell
(0.335, n=3843) is ENTIRELY 2026-06 — every other month of the era has no
oppose-D short rows (NOT MEASURED), because 2022–2025 have zero reads with a
clean D trend and 2026-07/08 shorts only ever sat with D=down (agree): 2026-07
with-D shorts 0.533 [0.522,0.544] n=7805; 2026-08 with-D shorts 0.683
[0.658,0.708] n=1323. So the direction of the effect is consistent across the
three months that have any D-trend coverage (with-D shorts hold 0.53–0.68;
oppose-D shorts hold 0.335) — but the sample is ONE QUARTER, not an era. It is
not a whole-era uptrend artifact in the simple sense; it is a coverage fact that
must travel with the number.

Second, the coverage fact itself: D-trend reads by year — 2022 0/530, 2023
0/707, 2024 0/674, 2025 0/665, **2026 84/517**. 4h-trend reads: 0/530, 0/707,
0/674, 0/665, **2026 57/517**. The store's per-contract daily/4h history only
yields 3 labelled swings for 2026 contracts (03-26/06-26/09-26/12-26). So the
gate's Q-B D/4h numbers are 2026 measurements, NOT era measurements — while the
1h state, which separates nothing (0.506 vs 0.513), is measured era-wide
(150–229 reads/year). Stated plainly in the gate report's cells would be
overstating the era; this file is the correction on the record.
